Privacy Statement

Pocket TMF App

Last Updated: 6th November 2026

Overview

Pocket TMF is a reference application that provides access to the Trial Master File (TMF) Reference Model Version 3.3.1, the TMF Standard Model V1 draft, and the Investigator Site File (ISF) Reference Model Version 1.0 (Provisional). This privacy statement explains how the app handles your information.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app works offline and does not:

  • Collect personal data such as names, email addresses, or contact information
  • Access your device's camera, microphone, or location services
  • Require user accounts or registration
  • Connect to external servers or databases, apart from the usage figures Google's ML Kit sends while Use Gemini Nano is on (see Ask the Model and Gemini Nano)
  • Track user behavior or collect usage analytics of its own
  • Access your contacts, photos, or other personal files

Data Usage

The app contains only:

  • Static reference content: pre-loaded TMF, Standard Model V1, and ISF data (zones, sections or record groups, artifacts, and their definitions)
  • Local app preferences: settings you configure (such as color scheme, theme, app icon, and navigation) are stored on your device with Jetpack DataStore
  • Favorites and personal notes: artifact favorites and notes you write (up to 240 characters each) are stored on your device with Jetpack DataStore and are not sent anywhere

Data Storage

All data in this app stays on your device:

  • Reference model data: the TMF, Standard Model V1, and ISF content is built into the app
  • User data: preferences, favorites, and personal notes are stored locally with Jetpack DataStore. Android backup is turned off, so this data is not copied to a new device
  • No cloud storage: nothing you enter in the app is transmitted to or stored on external servers. The app connects to the network only for Google's ML Kit usage figures while Use Gemini Nano is on. Links and email open in other apps on your device

Clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling the app, deletes preferences, favorites, and notes.

Ask the Model and Gemini Nano

Ask the Model finds where a document belongs in the TMF or ISF Reference Model. On its own it uses Smart Search, which runs entirely in the app. On Android devices that support it, Ask the Model can also use Gemini Nano, Google's on-device AI model, if you turn on Use Gemini Nano in Settings. It is off until you turn it on, and the app tells you what it involves before it turns on.

Gemini Nano runs on your device. Google's terms say your questions and Gemini Nano's answers stay on the device and are not sent to Google. The app reaches Gemini Nano through Google's ML Kit, and while Use Gemini Nano is on, ML Kit sends Google usage figures, which Google lists as:

  • the device's make, model, Android version and build, and its AI hardware
  • the app's package name and version
  • per-installation identifiers that Google says are not intended to identify you or your device
  • performance figures such as timings, the settings used, the size of each request and reply, and error codes
  • events such as starting the feature, model downloads and requests
  • the configured languages

Google says it uses these figures for diagnostics and usage analytics, sends them over HTTPS and does not pass them to third parties. Google handles them under its own privacy policy. Google's list is at developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure.

Turning Use Gemini Nano off stops the app using ML Kit.

Third-Party Services

This app does not include advertising networks or analytics platforms of its own. Its one third-party service is Google's ML Kit, used only while Use Gemini Nano is on, as described in Ask the Model and Gemini Nano.

Data Sharing

We do not collect any personal information, so we have none to share. While Use Gemini Nano is on, Google's ML Kit sends Google the usage figures described in Ask the Model and Gemini Nano.

Children's Privacy

This app is safe for users of all ages as it does not collect any personal information. Use Gemini Nano is off until it is turned on, and while it is off the app does not communicate with external services.

Data Security

The app does not collect or transmit personal information:

  • Preferences, favorites, and notes remain on your device
  • Apart from Google's ML Kit usage figures while Use Gemini Nano is on, the app does not connect to the network
  • Your privacy is protected by Android security features

Your Rights

The app does not collect personal information, so there is no personal data held by us to access, modify, or delete. On your device you can:

  • Change appearance settings at any time
  • Remove favorites and edit or delete notes inside the app
  • Delete preferences, favorites, and notes by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it
  • Turn Use Gemini Nano on or off at any time in Settings › Smart Search

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in app updates and will maintain our commitment to not collecting personal information.

Compliance

This app complies with:

  • Google Play Store Review Guidelines
  • Android privacy requirements
  • COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) as no personal information is collected
  • GDPR principles of data minimization (by collecting no personal data)

Note

This privacy statement reflects the current functionality of the Pocket TMF app as a reference tool that works offline. The app does not collect personal information. Preferences, favorites, and notes stay on the device. Apart from Google's ML Kit usage figures while Use Gemini Nano is on, the app does not transmit anything.
