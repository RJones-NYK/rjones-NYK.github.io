Privacy Statement

Pocket TMF App

Last Updated: 13 November 2026

Overview

Pocket TMF is a reference app for the Trial Master File (TMF) Reference Model version 3.3.1 and the Investigator Site File (ISF) Reference Model version 1.0 (Provisional). This statement explains what the app keeps and where.

In Short

Pocket TMF does not collect any information about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the app sends nothing to us or to anyone else. Everything you add stays on your device.

What the App Keeps on Your Device

  • Your settings: colour scheme, light or dark mode, app icon, haptics, tab bar layout, and whether you've seen the introduction.
  • Favorites: the Artifacts you mark.
  • My Notes: the notes you write on Artifacts.
  • Your Custom Structure, if you import one.

These are kept in the app's own storage on your device and are never sent anywhere. Like any app's data, they are included in your device's backups (iCloud or computer) if you have backups turned on.

Files You Choose to Open or Share

  • Custom Structure import reads only the CSV file you pick.
  • Export creates a CSV file and opens the iOS share sheet. You decide where it goes next (Files, Mail, AirDrop and so on). The app doesn't send it anywhere itself.

What the App Doesn't Do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party services.
  • No access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.
  • No connection to any server. The reference content is built into the app.

Links

A few screens link to CDISC, InnovationToolKit, LinkedIn and the support email. Tapping a link opens Safari or Mail, outside the app, and that site's own privacy policy applies.

Removing Your Information

  • Delete a note's text to remove the note.
  • Remove Favorites one at a time, or all at once with Clear All.
  • Clear your Custom Structure in the Custom Structure screen.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it has stored.

Children

Pocket TMF is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information and doesn't connect to any outside service.

Changes to This Statement

This statement is updated whenever the app changes what it keeps. The date at the top shows the latest version.
